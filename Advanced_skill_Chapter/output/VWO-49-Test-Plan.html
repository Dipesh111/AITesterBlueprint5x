1. Scope & Objectives

In scope

  • Visibility and ordering of “Sign in using SSO” and “Sign in with Passkey”.
  • Successful and failed SSO authentication.
  • Successful passkey authentication and fallback guidance.
  • Unsupported browser/device behavior, security, accessibility, responsiveness, and compatibility.
  • Regression checks for existing Google and email/password login options.

Out of scope

  • Passkey registration, covered by a separate story.
  • SSO provider configuration.

Objective: Determine whether the new login choices are usable, securely integrated, positioned correctly, compatible with supported environments, and do not regress existing authentication methods.

4. Test Data & Environment

  • Data: Synthetic valid/invalid SSO identities, authorized and unauthorized accounts, passkey-enabled devices, passkey-free profiles, and existing-login regression accounts.
  • Environment / flags: Staging URL and feature/configuration flag, OAuth/SAML test tenants, redirect URIs, browser/OS/device matrix, and passkey authenticators; all to be confirmed.
  • Roles / permissions: Authorized and unauthorized SSO identities, existing dashboard account, and logged-in/logged-out states.

5. Risks & Assumptions

  • Assumptions: VWO-49 is the intended ticket; passkey registration and provider configuration are separate; staging is the validation environment.
  • Risks: SSO may vary by provider/MFA; passkey behavior depends on browser, OS, and hardware; exact UI, copy, session, and rollback behavior is not stated; earlier automated runs were blocked.
